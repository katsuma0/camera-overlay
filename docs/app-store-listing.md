**Description**

Same Spot is a camera for timelapses that take weeks or months. You pick a spot and take one photo. Every time you come back, that first photo sits faintly over the camera so you can line up the shot and take it from the same place.

I made it because I wanted a timelapse of my backyard through the seasons and couldn't frame the photo the same way twice.

How it works:
- Make a project for a spot. A garden bed, a tree, a window, a street corner.
- Take the original. It becomes the guide.
- Set reminders for the times you want. Morning and night works well.
- Line up the guide and shoot. Slide to make it fainter or stronger, or line up with your last photo instead.
- When you have enough photos, make a video at 6 to 30 photos per second, then save it or share it.

Every photo in a project uses the same lens and the same orientation as the original, so a landscape project stays landscape.

There are no accounts, ads or tracking. Your photos stay on your phone.

## App Review notes

No account or sign-in. The app needs a camera, so please test on a device.

1. On Projects, tap +, type a name, tap Next.
2. The Camera tab opens. Take the original photo and tap Use Photo.
3. Take another photo. The original shows faintly over the camera to line up with.
4. On Projects, open the project and tap Make Timelapse. It needs two photos.

Reminders are local notifications set on each project.
